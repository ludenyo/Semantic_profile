Tech spotlight

Why I admire QGIS

QGIS is a powerful open-source Geographic Information System (GIS) that turns location data into clear, meaningful maps. It allows users to organise information in layers, such as roads, buildings, boundaries, and environmental features, so they can see how places and data relate to one another.

I admire QGIS because it brings analysis, visual design, and problem-solving together. It can help people explore questions about their communities, plan projects, and communicate useful geographic insights. As I learn frontend development, QGIS inspires me to create digital experiences that make complex information simple, accessible, and useful.
